## Context

See proposal.md. The growth page's tiers were rewritten in this unreleased edition; this Change finishes them.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Running text is English; Chinese names have one place each, the tables and the list of bands.
- The tier, evidence, assessment and promotion sections and the requirements agree.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any change to what a grade or a tier means.

## Decisions

- **English names.** apprentice, associate, junior, intermediate, senior, staff, expert, chief; bands tools, craft,
  method, direction, as the grade table already pairs them. The requirement that lists the bands gives each Chinese
  name beside its English one, since that requirement is where the names are defined.
- **Scenario titles keep their Chinese.** OpenSpec identifies a scenario by its title, and a renamed scenario is a
  removed one; their bodies use the English names.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Readers who know the Chinese names find them in the grade table, the first table on the page.
