## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Promotion follows sustained evidence and carried responsibility

A member SHALL NOT be promoted unless they have reached S or A at their grade in
at least two consecutive periods and already carry one of the next grade's
responsibilities, other than leading a team while below senior, in their actual
work, read from the same delegation records as S; the count SHALL restart after
a grade change. Entering senior or expert SHALL also require the evidence the
guidelines name. Years, calendar time, and a single high score SHALL NOT replace
any of this evidence.

#### Scenario: A member is proposed for 高级

- **WHEN** a promotion to senior is reviewed
- **THEN** the record shows, in each of at least two different pieces of work, a
  method the member designed that another person adopted, that ran a full cycle,
  and that held when conditions changed; the methods need not be the same
- **AND** it shows, at least once, that the member predicted, before the result
  was known, where a piece of their work was most likely to fail, that the
  result bore the prediction out, and that another person used the member's
  conclusion directly
- **AND** after the first year of use, the department reviews how many
  consecutive periods promotion requires.
