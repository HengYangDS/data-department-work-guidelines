## Why

The repository has a locally validated documentation-adopter closeout history,
but its candidate baseline lacks the admitted carrier, claim, and archive
transition. A prior unleased lane holds related work; it cannot be used as
current governance authority.

## What Changes

- Establish an explicit material-path declaration for this documentation adopter.
- Rebuild the historical closeout and reconciliation evidence under a leased,
  current Change.
- Use official OpenSpec archive transitions and repeat HEAD-bound local proof.
- Keep forge publication, hosted rendering, and organizational adoption outside
  this Change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `repository-governance`: require a dated official archive carrier and a fresh
  local proof before a documentation-adopter closeout can be presented.

## Impact

This affects ETHOS adopter metadata, proof evidence, OpenSpec carriers, and the
repository-governance specification. It does not alter `guidelines.md`.
