## Why

The book's reader, reading chapter 29 against DDWG 10.0.0, found eight places in the growth page where the text is
unclear or inconsistent, and where the book could not follow it without writing its own reading. One of them is a
fidelity gap older than 10.0.0: the evidence for entering senior asks the member to have named, before the result,
where the work was most likely to fail, while the committee text the owner approved said the prediction was borne
out.

## What Changes

- Say that adjacent tiers differ in kind, not in amount (the owner's words), instead of calling each tier a different
  kind of contribution.
- Read a prediction the member's own row asks for toward B, and count a prediction toward A only when it shows the
  item the next grade adds.
- Count a late result once it is accepted and used; read work whose delivery, acceptance or use falls after the
  period's end in the period of use; treat only a result due in the period and still undelivered at its end as no
  result; name "no accepted and used result" the same way everywhere.
- Read A's value at the scope of the piece of work it came from.
- Ask, for entering senior, that the result bore out the member's prediction of where the work was most likely to
  fail, as the approved committee text said.
- Name the period after a C as "that next period"; define a full cycle once, for a responsibility, a method or a
  standard.
- Cut 10.0.1.
