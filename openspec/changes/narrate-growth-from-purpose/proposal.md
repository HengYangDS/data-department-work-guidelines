## Why

On 2026-11-28 the department head judged the companion book's chapter on goals and recognition
mechanical and set its principles: guide members upward rather than set up rules, help them grow
toward clear goals, and never treat people as objects, while still explaining the eight grades and
the five tiers. The book points to these guidelines for every condition, so the guidelines must tell
grades and tiers in the same order. The growth page opens on mechanism, reads the tiers from D,
states the department's support after the shortfall, and gives a tier's purpose only at the end of
the tier section, after its rules.

## What Changes

- On the growth page, state the purpose first: grades and tiers exist to help each member grow, a
  tier shows each member where they stand at their own grade and where to go next, and what they ask
  is declared before each period. The sentence on pay, rewards and sanctions moves beside the
  decisions a tier informs.
- Explain the tiers starting from B: B the grade's expectation, C the passing line, D below it, then
  A and S toward the next grade, with the next step each points to. D's conditions keep their
  precedence in how a period is read.
- After a C, state first what the department provides (a goal, a named coach and an in-person check
  in the middle of the period); then the improvement period that the member and the decision owner
  agree, its training or coaching first.
- Make the member the subject of what they do, learn and need, and give the reason for the boundary
  rule before the rule.
- Elsewhere under `docs/`, give a rule about people its purpose before its limits where it lacks
  one: the charter's four boundaries, and the communication page's rule on meeting invitations.
- Record the change under `Unreleased` in the Changelog.

No grade, tier, evidence rule, assessment step, promotion or hiring rule changes in meaning. Each
normative statement before the change has a counterpart after it; the list, with the statements
that were joined or split, stays with the lane's evidence. The places outside this repository that
use the grades and tiers are listed there too, for their own changes after a release.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The accepted requirements stay as they are, and the Change sets the official `skip_specs`
marker. The work-practice scenario "Assessors decide between adjacent tiers" reads "after D's
conditions, which are read first"; the page keeps "D's conditions are read first" in how a period is
read, after the explanation that starts from B.

## Impact

The growth page, one sentence each on the charter and the communication page, and the Changelog. Out
of scope: the rules themselves, the specifications, the book and the recruitment repositories, which
change in their own sessions, and a version or release.
