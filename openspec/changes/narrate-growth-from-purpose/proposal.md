## Why

On 2026-11-28 the owner judged the companion book's chapter on goals and recognition mechanical and
set its principles: guide members upward rather than set up rules, help them grow toward clear
goals, and never treat people as objects, while still explaining the eight grades and the five
tiers. The book points to these guidelines for every condition, so the guidelines must tell grades
and tiers in the same order. The growth page opens on mechanism, reads the tiers from D, states the
department's support after the shortfall, and gives a tier's purpose only after every rule.

## What Changes

- On the growth page, state the purpose first: a tier shows each member where they stand at their
  own grade and where to go next, recognizes good work, and rests on a basis declared before the
  period.
- Read the tiers from B: B the grade's expectation, C the passing line, D below it, then A and S
  toward the next grade, with the next step each points to. D's conditions keep their precedence in
  how a period is read.
- Where a period reads C or D, state what the department provides (a goal, a coach, an in-person
  check in the middle of the period, and a written improvement agreement with its training or
  coaching) before the conditions that follow.
- Make the member the subject of what they do, learn and need, and give the reason for the boundary
  rule before the rule.
- Elsewhere under `docs/`, reword each sentence that treats people as objects.
- Record the change under `Unreleased` in the Changelog.

No grade, tier, evidence rule, assessment step, promotion or hiring rule changes in meaning. Every
normative statement keeps one counterpart; the before and after list stays with the lane's
evidence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The accepted requirements stay as they are, and the Change sets the official `skip_specs`
marker. One work-practice scenario pins wording that this Change moves: "Assessors decide between
adjacent tiers" reads "after D's conditions, which are read first", and the page keeps "D's
conditions are read first" in how a period is read, after the narration that starts at B.

## Impact

The growth page, any sentence the sweep of the other pages catches, and the Changelog. Out of
scope: the rules themselves, the specifications, the book, which changes in its own lane, and a
version or release.
