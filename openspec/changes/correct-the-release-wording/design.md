## Context

See proposal.md. The 9.0.0 draft marked each bare imperative with "you" and a modal word. Where a
paragraph names a narrower actor, or where the step is a label rather than a duty, that form misreads
the source. The quoted definition of fidelity, clarity and elegance stays word for word.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Every corrected sentence keeps its words and changes only its actor or its word of force.

**Non-Goals:**

- New duties. "Important" in boundary 3 stays for the owner to settle.

## Decisions

- **The actor the paragraph names.** The Agent's conditions on the collaboration page bind the Agent;
  the responses to an acceptance bind the acceptor; the review rhythm binds its owner.
- **Restrictions as prohibitions.** "May … only when" reads as a permission; "must not … unless"
  states the restriction the charter's words define.
- **Steps are not duties.** The working loop names steps that are not mandatory stages, so they carry
  no word of force; the data page's questions keep theirs, which apply when a question applies.
