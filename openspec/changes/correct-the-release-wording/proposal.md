## Why

Before the 9.0.0 tag, the films' steward read the draft against v8.0.0 and the two films. Several
sentences carried the wrong actor or a force that misreads the source: Agent and acceptor duties
addressed to every reader, a must joined to a default, permissions that were meant as prohibitions,
and the working loop's steps read as duties. Six wording faults from the films' ledger were still
open.

## What Changes

- Restore the actor where a paragraph names a narrower one: the Agent, the person responsible for
  the affected work, the owner of the review rhythm, and the acceptor.
- Align the Agent's stop condition with the single stop rule: a consequence counts whether or not it
  is irreversible.
- Write a default as should; write each "may … only when" restriction as "must not … unless"; the
  chain of trust "must not" skip a link.
- Return the working loop's steps to their names, without a word of force, since they are not
  mandatory stages.
- Give the third non-negotiable boundary its word of force, and make evading a necessary record by
  calling a task L0 a must-not.
- Correct four wordings: "inflate the wording", "overridden", "These guidelines", and "the decision
  owner" for high-risk approval.
