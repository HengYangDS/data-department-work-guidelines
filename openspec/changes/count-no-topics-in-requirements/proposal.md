## Why

Two accepted requirements count the department topics: "Entry and seven topics SHALL retain …"
and "DDWG SHALL select its current entrypoints, seven department topics …". The page on grades,
tiers, and growth is an eighth normative source, so the count no longer matches the declared
sources, and every later page would make it wrong again (found by the lane review).

## What Changes

- The two requirements name the topics without a number; the declared normative sources remain the
  one list of them.
- The scenario that checks the retired guidelines names the current topics that replaced them,
  which the new page is not.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `guidance-discovery`: "Task routes preserve the work-quality contract" names every topic instead
  of seven.
- `quality`: "DDWG source selects one English reader corpus" names the department topics instead
  of seven.

## Impact

Two requirements' text; no page changes. Their titles stay.
