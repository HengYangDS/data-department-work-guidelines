## Why

On 2026-11-24 the owner set the tier scale after an expert discussion: B is the performance a grade expects, C is
the passing line, and D falls below it; adjacent tiers differ in kind, not in amount; S and A differ in the value
and reach of the work, not in a count of evidence; and the scale is the same everywhere the department uses it.
The page's tiers do not say this, so they are restated as five tiers, each a different kind of contribution read
from one kind of record.

## What Changes

- Read tiers on five letters, S, A, B, C, and D, each with its Chinese name, and read each boundary between
  adjacent tiers as one yes-or-no question about one kind of record: an accepted and used result for each
  requirement (C), the member's own key judgment in each (B), an item of the capability the next grade adds (A),
  and one of the next grade's responsibilities delegated and carried for a full cycle (S). A and S also rest on the
  value their users record. D covers a requirement with no accepted and used result, an agreed improvement period
  that ended with a requirement not met, and two crossings of the same non-negotiable boundary in a period.
- State what follows a C and a D, how an improvement period is agreed, that a tier does not set pay and is not a
  sanction, and how 首席's A and S are stated before the period.
- Publish the tier definitions before each period, and review agreement at every boundary after the first two
  periods.
- Cut 10.0.0 in `VERSION` and the Changelog.
