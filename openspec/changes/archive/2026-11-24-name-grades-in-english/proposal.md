## Why

The guidelines are written in English, but the growth page's running text and the work-practice requirements name
grades in Chinese, such as "a 资深 who turns a method into a review rule". On 2026-11-24 the owner decided that the
running text names grades in English and that Chinese names stay in the tables. A whole-page read of the 10.0.0 text
also found places where two assessors would read the same records differently.

## What Changes

- Name grades and bands in English in the growth page's running text and the work-practice requirements; the grade
  table, the tier and function tables, the list of bands and two scenario titles keep their Chinese.
- Ask A's question as the A row does: the same added item in at least two pieces of work.
- Read S when its responsibility's cycle ends, or when its result is used if that is later; bound D's improvement-period
  condition to the period; reconcile only differing tiers; exclude leading a team below senior from the responsibility
  promotion asks for; let a single piece of work give a tier only together with the period's records.
- Restate the 10.0.0 Changelog entry to the final text.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `work-practice`: the requirements "A piece of work and a member's performance are judged separately", "One
  professional sequence of eight grades", "A tier describes performance at the member's own grade" and "Promotion
  follows sustained evidence and carried responsibility".

## Impact

`docs/grow.md`, `openspec/specs/work-practice/spec.md` (through the delta) and `CHANGELOG.md`, in the same 10.0.0
release.
