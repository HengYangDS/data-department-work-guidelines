## Why

On 2026-11-21 the owner set one definition of fidelity, clarity and elegance (信、达、雅) for all writing meant
for a person, to be cited and never reworded, and asked that the guidelines' own wording meet it. An independent
reading of the published guidelines found that they also state one stop rule eight ways, use "admission" for two
different acts on data, leave the force of a bare imperative undefined, qualify duties with four risk words that no
level defines, and leave "critical management chains" undefined, besides smaller faults of reference and wording.
The owner settled the five questions that only the owner could answer on 2026-11-22; the maintainer settled the
others from the text.

## What Changes

- The communication topic quotes the single definition of fidelity, clarity and elegance word for word; the
  charter, the README and the writing requirement link to it instead of restating it.
- The stop rule is stated once, in the charter, with every member-facing condition of its eight versions; a
  consequence counts whether or not it is irreversible. The other places link to it and keep only what they add.
- Every bare imperative carries must, must not, should or may, as the accepted requirement that covers it states;
  content no requirement states takes should, should not or may, and must or must not where it restates one of the four
  boundaries. Pointers to another section and illustrative cases stay unmarked. The charter defines should not beside
  the four words, and must and must not allow no departure.
- Data admission is named twice: asset admission for the eight conditions of a durable asset, and use admission for
  a specified use. An admitted asset may serve the uses its admission lists; any other use needs its own use
  admission.
- "Important work", "consequential delegation", "high-impact" and "important result or decision" become work at
  level L1 or L2. **BREAKING**: duties these words carried now apply to all L1 work.
- "Critical management chains" are defined as the data chains that feed key management decisions or reports.
- Smaller corrections: hard boundaries named as the four non-negotiable boundaries, the classification basis, the
  permission for every intended use, the agreed lifecycle state linked to the delivery states, projections that
  become a source of truth only by explicit authorized designation, weak structural signals as a description and not
  a condition, the end-to-end result of a task with its task lead, "tell apart" in the README, and repeated Agent
  work stated plainly.
