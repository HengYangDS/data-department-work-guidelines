## Why

Reading tiers on five letters added a sentence that a tier does not set pay and is not a sanction, with a
scenario for decisions that cite a tier. On 2026-11-24 the owner decided this need not be stressed; the page's
existing rule, that pay follows the grade and attainment and that rewards and sanctions may take the tier as one
of their grounds, says enough.

## What Changes

- Remove the added sentence from the growth page and the clause from the 10.0.0 Changelog entry, and reword the
  scenario "A tier would feed pay or a sanction" of the work-practice requirement "Tier assessment is evidenced and
  fair" so that it states only the existing rule.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `work-practice`: the requirement "Tier assessment is evidenced and fair" rewords its scenario "A tier would feed
  pay or a sanction" to the existing rule.

## Impact

`docs/grow.md`, `openspec/specs/work-practice/spec.md` (through the delta) and `CHANGELOG.md`, in the same 10.0.0
release.
