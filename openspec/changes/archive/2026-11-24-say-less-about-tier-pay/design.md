## Context

See proposal.md. The scenario and the sentence were added in the same unreleased edition by
`read-tiers-on-five-letters`; no released text ever held them.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- The page and the requirement say about pay only what the page said before 10.0.0.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any other change to the tiers.

## Decisions

- **Same release.** The removal ships with 10.0.0, so no published edition ever carried the sentence.
- **Reword, not remove, the scenario.** A MODIFIED requirement keeps every scenario of the current specification,
  so the scenario keeps its title and now states only the existing rule: pay follows the grade and attainment, and a
  decision takes the tier with its records as one of its grounds.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- None beyond the expert discussion's note that a tier may be read as a decision on employment; the existing rule on
  pay and sanctions answers it.
