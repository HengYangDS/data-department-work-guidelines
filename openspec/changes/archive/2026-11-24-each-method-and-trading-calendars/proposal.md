## Why

Two readers of the guidelines found wording that can be read two ways. "Entering senior" asks for "in at least two
different pieces of work, a method the member designed", which one assessor reads as one method used in two pieces of
work and another as a method in each piece; the owner chose the second on 2026-11-25. The data page names one thing
twice: "market calendars" where it lists sources of bias, and "trading calendars" where it assigns ownership.

## What Changes

- Say, for entering senior and in the scenario "A member is proposed for 高级", that each of at least two different
  pieces of work shows a method the member designed, and that the methods need not be the same.
- Give the borne-out prediction a sentence of its own on the page, so "each of" does not reach it, and name the same
  object in the page and the scenario: the member's conclusion, which another person used directly.
- State that promotion asks for one of the next grade's responsibilities, read from the same delegation records as S.
- Attach "below senior" to the member ("while below senior"), write entering expert with one subject and tense, and say
  that nothing replaces any of this evidence.
- Use "trading calendars" on the data page throughout.
- Cut 10.0.3.
