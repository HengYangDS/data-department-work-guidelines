## Context

See proposal.md. The owner asked for one final consensus across the sessions that use the scale (2026-11-24 17:27).
Four expert papers (performance practice, measurement, employment compliance, a data team lead) and two
independent checks of the consensus led to the scale here; the owner approved it at 18:17 and named D 不及格. He
then decided (18:24, 18:25) that the page states the new scale as it is, with no transition from any earlier one.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Each tier is a different kind of contribution, and each boundary is one question a second assessor answers from
  the records.
- S and A differ in kind and in the reach of their value; their evidence counts do not tell them apart.
- One definition, cited by the book, the films, the recruitment playbook and HR material.

**Non-Goals:**

- The grade table, promotion, and work scores do not change.
- No percentage or score is attached to a tier.

## Decisions

- **Two steps within the grade, two toward the next.** The grade table's two columns, "What the grade shows" and
  "Responsibility it can carry", give the steps: within the grade, whether each requirement has an accepted and
  used result (C) and whether the key judgment is the member's own (B); toward the next grade, an item of the
  capability the next grade adds (A), and one of its responsibilities delegated and carried for a full cycle (S).
  What the member's own row already asks counts toward B only, so a 资深's review rule is not read as A.
- **Value is recorded by users.** A and S hold only with a record from the users of the result: who used it and
  what it changed. A's value is within the member's own work; S's reaches the scope of the delegated
  responsibility.
- **No percentages.** Tiers differ in kind; a percentage is an evenly spaced amount, invites averaging and half
  tiers, and is absolute while a tier is read at the member's grade.
- **Pay and sanctions.** A tier does not set pay and is not a sanction; decisions on pay, position or employment
  follow the company's published rules and the procedures the law requires, with the tier and its records as one
  factual ground. A boundary crossed twice in a period reads D because the boundaries are the conditions under
  which others can rely on the member.
- **Edition.** The scale's letters and their meanings change incompatibly, so the release takes a major increment,
  10.0.0. Dated 2026-11-25, the day the release is expected to be tagged; the date moves with the tag.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Assessors may avoid C, so that B becomes a default; B and C each state their records requirement by
  requirement, and the review after the first two periods reads agreement at every boundary.
- At two boundaries of the grade table (初级 to 中级, 资深 to 专家) the next row adds little in writing, so A and S are
  harder to read there; a later revision of the grade table addresses it.
- 「不及格」 may be read as unfit for the post in an employment sense; the page states that a tier is not a sanction and
  that such decisions follow published rules and the law.
