## Why

The films' reader, reading DDWG 10.0.1, found that the scenario "A member is proposed for 高级" puts the borne-out
prediction under "in at least two different pieces of work", while the page asks for it at least once; the fault came
in with 10.0.1. A whole-section read of the page's Promotion section against the specification then found that the
requirement drops "read from the same delegation records as S", which the page and the approved consensus keep, and
four places where the requirement's wording can be read two ways.

## What Changes

- State the requirement as the page does: no promotion unless the two periods hold and the member already carries the
  next grade's responsibility, read from the same delegation records as S; entering senior or expert also requires the
  evidence the guidelines name; nothing replaces this evidence.
- Give the borne-out prediction its own line in the scenario, at least once, with the conclusion another person used.
- Say once, on the page and in the scenario, that after the first year of use the department reviews how many
  consecutive periods promotion requires.
- Cut 10.0.2.
