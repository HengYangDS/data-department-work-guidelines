## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Promotion follows sustained evidence and carried responsibility

A member SHALL NOT be promoted unless they have reached S or A at their grade in
at least two consecutive periods and already carry the next grade's
responsibility, other than leading a team below senior, in their actual work,
read from the same delegation records as S; the count SHALL restart after a
grade change. Entering senior or expert SHALL also require the evidence the
guidelines name. Years, calendar time, and a single high score SHALL NOT replace
this evidence.

#### Scenario: A member is proposed for 高级

- **WHEN** a promotion to senior is reviewed
- **THEN** the record shows, in at least two different pieces of work, a method
  the member designed that another person adopted, that ran a full cycle, and
  that held when conditions changed
- **AND** it shows, at least once, that the result bore out the member's
  prediction, made before the result, of where the work was most likely to
  fail, and that another person used the work's conclusion directly
- **AND** after the first year of use, the department reviews how many
  consecutive periods promotion requires.
