## Context

See proposal.md. One reader read the page's Promotion section and the specification's Promotion requirement together
in one pass and gave thirteen findings with exact wording; this Change takes those that bring the specification to the
page's meaning.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- The specification and the page say the same thing about promotion, in wording that reads one way.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any change to what promotion requires. Whether "in at least two different pieces of work, a method the member
  designed" means one method or a method in each piece is a question of meaning older than 10.0.1; it is left as it is
  and put to the owner. The wording "the next grade's responsibility" and "other than leading a team below senior" is
  used across the page and is left as it is.

## Decisions

- **Edition.** Corrections that bring the specification to the page: a patch, 10.0.2.
- **Delegation records.** The approved consensus of 2026-11-24 says promotion reads the same delegation record as S;
  the page says so and the requirement now does too.
- **Modal form.** "SHALL NOT be promoted unless" carries the page's "may be promoted only when"; "SHALL be promoted
  only after" could be read as a duty to promote.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- None for a member: the page has said this since 10.0.0, and no period has yet been assessed on it.
