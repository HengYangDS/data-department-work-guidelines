## Why

The charter's third non-negotiable boundary reads "Important judgments, decisions, changes, and
acceptances must have an accountable person." "Important" admits two readings: every risk level,
or only L1 and L2 work. The same page says risk determines the form, not whether a duty exists, and
that every task must meet the four boundaries. The films' steward asked which reading holds (item
31 of the films' ledger); the owner decided on 2026-11-22 that the boundary binds every level.

## What Changes

- Drop "Important" from the third boundary: judgments, decisions, changes, and acceptances must
  have an accountable person.
- The 9.1.0 Changelog records the correction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The accepted requirement that L1 and L2 results record their accountable owner is the form
of this duty and stays as it is; the correction uses the official no-spec marker.

## Impact

One sentence of the charter and the 9.1.0 Changelog.
