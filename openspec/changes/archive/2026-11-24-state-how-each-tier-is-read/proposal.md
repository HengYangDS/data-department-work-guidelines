## Why

Independent readers of the 10.0.0 tier section found places where two assessors applying it to the same records
would reach different tiers: the summary claimed one kind of record per boundary where A and S need two; B and C
could both fail without D holding; a 学徒's guidance was neither the member's own judgment nor another's; A could rest
on one piece of work against the evidence rule; work not yet due could read D; and assessors at B and S had no rule.
The meaning of the tiers does not change; how each is read from records is stated once.

## What Changes

- Restate the summary sentence: D's conditions first, then one yes-or-no question per tier, with A and S also needing
  their users' record of value.
- C: at least one accepted and used result for every touched requirement. B: meets C, and each has one with the
  member's own key judgment. A: the same added item in at least two pieces of work. S: a responsibility the next row
  adds, other than leading a team below 高级.
- Define a requirement as an item of work or capability in the grade's row, not a limit; read work in the period in
  which it is used; read the row's own guidance, acceptance and review as the member's own for B; read S in the period
  in which its cycle ends; have 首席's A and S each stated in writing before the period; count one event as one
  crossing; name the test (C's or B's) for each requirement of an improvement period.
- In the evidence and assessment sections: name users' records of value as evidence; let a borne-out prediction count
  toward A; limit "one strong piece" to A; add the other-domain assessor when either assessor reads S; reconcile B
  against S, when every assessor answers A's question no, as adjacent tiers.
- Make the work-practice scenarios say the same; say that a grade's requirements are the items of its row.
