## Context

See proposal.md. The charter is the normative statement; the README introduces the guidelines and
must agree with it.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- One meaning in both places: the same three products with the same three properties.

**Non-Goals:**

- A new duty. The properties already follow from the four non-negotiable boundaries.

## Decisions

- **All three products carry the three properties.** The charter's boundaries already require them of
  actions: an important change has an accountable person, who can explain it; a claim that a result
  is complete or correct needs current evidence, so it can be checked; and no action crosses its
  applicable limits.
- **"Relied on" rather than "used".** One uses a judgment or a data asset but takes an action; each
  of the three can be relied on within its limits, which is what "reliable" in the charter says.
- **The charter keeps its sentence.** It already names the three products; the README's sentence now
  spells out what their reliability consists of.
