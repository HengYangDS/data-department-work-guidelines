# Proposal

## Why

The previous migration from the unified Chinese guideline to the English topic
pages was accepted using a chapter-to-owner map. A clause-level comparison now
shows that this map overstated fidelity: several distinct work obligations and
reader-facing stop and verification cues were weakened or lost. The evolution
page also rejects every fixed review cadence, although a periodic feedback floor
is needed to detect quiet drift and the owner has explicitly required it.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING** Restore the missing work-quality duties in their existing English
  topic owners. In particular, preserve problem decomposition coverage, the
  promotion boundary for exploratory data and code, bounded Agent parallelism
  and completion reporting, and the three distinct values of a completed task.
- Restore a small task-oriented start, stop, and verification route without
  recreating the old six-card inventory or a root monolith. A reader should not
  have to assemble a safety boundary from unrelated pages.
- Replace the blanket “no fixed calendar” rule with event-triggered checks and
  a proportionate periodic floor for work calibration, systemic signals, and
  rule retirement. The obligation is to make and record a useful judgment in an
  existing carrier, not to hold a new meeting or produce an activity report.
- Record a clause-level disposition against the original unified guideline:
  retained with a current owner, repaired, or deliberately retired with a
  reason. Do not treat section mapping, document length, or green formatting as
  proof of semantic coverage.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `guidance-discovery`: the task route and seven normative owners must retain
  unique duties and actionable stop and verification cues; a bounded periodic
  review floor replaces the prior unconditional rejection of fixed cadence.

## Impact

The Change may revise the existing task map and normative topic pages, the
`guidance-discovery` specification, release Changelog, and tests that protect
routing and source ownership. It adds no second normative tree, root
guideline, template library,
claim ledger, or repository-specific lifecycle. The old mandatory meeting
length and eleven-section document template are not restored. Historical
Changes remain historical, and neither a passing repository check nor this
repair certifies team adoption. The separate offline verification Change keeps
its own intent and tasks; this Change must join its accepted source before the
next major release is tagged or its offline bundle is qualified for that final
source revision.
