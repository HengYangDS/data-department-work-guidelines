## Context

The owner's words of 2026-11-22 set the direction: work and talent are judged separately, each by
its own standard; a talent judgment rests only on the work a person did and is never a judgment of
the person; tiers are relative to the member's own grade; grade names and requirements may be
public, without amounts, headcounts, or names. An expert committee convened for the companion book
debated the scheme in two rounds; the owner approved its consensus with the maintainer's
amendments and approved this page's wording.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- One source for the department's grades and tiers, which the book and the recruitment documents
  derive from or cite.
- Positive wording: each tier states what the work shows and what comes next.

**Non-Goals:**

- Pay bands, headcounts, or any person's tier, which stay out of a public repository.
- Changing the five-level work score or its requirement.
- Anchors for each tier. The page defines each tier relative to the grade's written requirements and
  gives one department-wide evidence list; anchors drawn from real records are written after the first
  period of use, when there are records to draw them from.

## Decisions

- **Three scales, one direction of evidence.** A work score, a tier, and a grade each answer one
  question; evidence flows only upward, so no single piece of work sets a tier.
- **Tiers relative to the grade.** One definition per tier across the department, read at the
  height the grade sets; the absolute level belongs to the grade alone, so every member has a goal
  within reach and no fact is held twice.
- **No usual band per grade.** An interval would become a quota; a grade states only its bar.
- **The committee's four open items take the maintainer's proposals, approved with the page:** two
  grades for 学徒 and 专员; S reviewed after two periods and recorded as an event if assessors
  cannot tell it from A; a tier read against the requirements of its time; two periods reviewed
  after the first year.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A tier read as a label of the person] → The page and the requirements state what a tier is not,
  and a tier travels only with its period, scope, and record.
- [Assessors drift apart] → Two assessors, an outside assessor for S, and a published agreement
  rate.
