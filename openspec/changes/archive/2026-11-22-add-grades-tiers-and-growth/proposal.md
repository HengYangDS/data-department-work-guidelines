## Why

The guidelines judge work but say nothing about how a member's work over time is judged, how
members move between grades, or how assessment stays fair. Without a stated standard there is no
shared direction, assessment cannot be checked, and recognition rests on impressions. The owner
asked for a standard for judging work and talent, a professional sequence of grades, and S to E
tiers, serving long-term direction, recognition, fair assessment, development, and selection.

## What Changes

- Add `docs/grow.md`, "Grades, Tiers, and Growth": three scales on one evidence chain (work score,
  tier, grade); one professional sequence of eight grades in four bands (器, 术, 法, 道); six tiers
  that describe a period's work at the member's own grade, with B as the grade's bar; the evidence
  a tier needs; a fair assessment procedure with no quota; promotion and the two crossings; hiring.
- In `docs/evolve.md`, state that a work score judges one piece of work, and link to the new page.
- Add a route to the documentation map and the page to the normative sources.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `work-practice`: six requirements added for the two standards, the sequence, tiers, fair
  assessment, promotion, and hiring. No existing requirement changes.

## Impact

A compatible addition of duties for assessment, promotion, and hiring; no existing duty changes.
Released as 9.1.0. The grade names and requirements are public; no amount, headcount, or name of a
person enters the repository.
