## Why

The growth page's summary sentence says each boundary between adjacent tiers asks about one kind of record, and
names for C only an accepted and used result for each requirement. D also holds when an agreed improvement period
ended with a requirement not met or the same non-negotiable boundary was crossed twice in a period, and D overrides
the other rows; read alone, the sentence drops two of D's three conditions. It also frames S as the step after A,
while S does not require A. A reader of the films found both on 2026-11-24.

## What Changes

- Restate the summary sentence so that C asks for an accepted and used result for each requirement with none of
  D's other conditions holding, and A and S are two questions asked of a period that meets B.
- State the same in the work-practice scenario "Assessors decide between adjacent tiers".

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `work-practice`: the requirement "A tier describes performance at the member's own grade" restates its scenario
  "Assessors decide between adjacent tiers".

`docs/grow.md` and `openspec/specs/work-practice/spec.md` (through the delta), in the same 10.0.0 release.
