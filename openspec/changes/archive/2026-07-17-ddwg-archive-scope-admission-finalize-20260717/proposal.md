## Why

The documentation repository already contains the normative `guidelines.md`
system, but its current candidate baseline needs a fresh, governed local proof
closeout. A foreign unleased lane is evidence only; it cannot supply current
mutation authority.

## What Changes

- Declare the documentation adopter's material paths explicitly.
- Establish an active closeout carrier, claim, and Chronicle in a leased lane.
- Archive that carrier only through official OpenSpec, then open a separate
  reconciliation carrier for the post-archive proof head.
- Keep forge publication, hosted rendering, and organizational adoption outside
  this local closure.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `repository-governance`: subject=ddwg-local-adopter-closeout;
  reuse=extend; change=add; facet:lifecycle=archive,proof;
  facet:surface=openspec,claim,evidence; facet:authority=openspec,claim,evidence.

## Out of Scope

- `guidelines.md` content or a second normative guide.
- GitLab/GitHub publication, hosted rendering, and organizational adoption.
- Foreign or owner-unknown Work Lane changes.

## Impact

This affects ETHOS adopter metadata, local evidence, OpenSpec lifecycle, and
one repository-governance requirement. It does not alter `guidelines.md`.
