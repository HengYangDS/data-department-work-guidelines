## Context

See proposal.md. The communication topic holds the department's single definition of fidelity,
clarity, and elegance; every other page cites it.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- The first sentence of fidelity names who does the work as well as who is responsible.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any other change to the definition, including the wording of elegance.

## Decisions

- **Wording.** "who does the work and who is responsible". A shorter "who acts" was rejected: it can
  be read as whoever takes some action or a position, and it does not separate the doer from the
  accountable person clearly.
- **Edition.** The change adds one item that writing keeps intact. A text that already named both
  people meets the new sentence; a text that left the doer out now falls short of it, which is the
  purpose of the change. The publication requirement classifies a compatible addition to a duty as
  a minor increment, as 9.1.0 did for its new duties, so the edition is 9.2.0.
- **Date.** 2026-11-23, the day the release is expected to be tagged.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A reader who quotes the earlier sentence keeps the shorter list. The Changelog names the change so
  that quotations can be brought in line.
