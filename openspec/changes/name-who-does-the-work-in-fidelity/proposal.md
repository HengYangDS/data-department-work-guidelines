## Why

The department's definition of fidelity, in the communication topic, lists what writing keeps
intact: meaning, facts, conditions and limits, who is responsible, and what is uncertain. It folds
the person who does a piece of work into the person accountable for it, and the two are often
different people: an Agent drafts and a maintainer is accountable, or one member does the work that
another accepts. On 2026-11-23 the owner decided that the definition names both.

## What Changes

- In the communication topic, the first sentence of fidelity names who does the work beside who is
  responsible: "meaning, facts, conditions and limits, who does the work and who is responsible,
  and what is uncertain all stay intact." Clarity, elegance, and the order in which they prevail
  are unchanged.
- Cut 9.2.0 in `VERSION` and the Changelog. The unreleased CI entry (keeping the download cache on
  each runner) is released with it.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The requirement "Writing serves fidelity, clarity, and elegance" binds writing to the
communication topic's single definition, so its text does not change; the change uses the official
no-spec marker.

## Impact

`docs/communicate.md`, `VERSION`, and `CHANGELOG.md`. A reader who cites the first sentence of
fidelity follows the new wording.
