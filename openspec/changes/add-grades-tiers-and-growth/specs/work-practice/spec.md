## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A piece of work and a member's performance are judged separately

The department SHALL judge one piece of work by a work score, a member's work
over one period by a tier read at the member's own grade, and the responsibility
a member has shown they can reliably carry by a grade. All three SHALL rest only
on work the member did and SHALL NOT judge personality, background, loyalty, or
worth. A tier SHALL NOT be set from one piece of work, nor a grade from one
period.

#### Scenario: One strong piece of work is offered as a tier

- **WHEN** a reviewer proposes a tier from a single strong deliverable
- **THEN** the work keeps its work score and the tier rests on the period's
  records
- **AND** a tier does not change a work score, and a grade does not change a
  tier.

### Requirement: One professional sequence of eight grades

Every member SHALL be on one professional sequence of eight grades in four
bands: 器 (学徒, 专员), 术 (初级, 中级), 法 (高级, 资深), and 道 (专家, 首席). A
grade SHALL state what it shows and the responsibility it can carry, and SHALL
NOT be a management level. A member below 高级 SHALL NOT lead a team, and a 首席
SHALL NOT lead one.

#### Scenario: Years in a grade are offered as a reason to promote

- **WHEN** a manager cites time in a grade as the reason for a promotion
- **THEN** the guidelines treat years as a usual reading, not a requirement
- **AND** "independently" means the next person can use the delivered work as
  it is, with what was not checked stated.

### Requirement: A tier describes performance at the member's own grade

A tier SHALL describe a member's work in one period at their own grade, with
one department-wide definition per tier read at the height the grade sets; B
SHALL be the grade's bar. Tiers at different grades SHALL NOT be compared. A
tier SHALL be read against the grade requirements in force when it was
assessed. Pay SHALL follow the grade and attainment; rewards and sanctions MAY
take the tier as one of their grounds.

#### Scenario: Two members' tiers at different grades are compared

- **WHEN** someone compares an apprentice's S with a staff member's B
- **THEN** the guidelines read each tier only against its own grade's bar
- **AND** the tier is used to show the member where they stand and where to go
  next, to recognize good work, and to plan development.

### Requirement: Tier assessment is evidenced and fair

The same grade and the same evidence SHALL lead to the same tier. S and A SHALL
each rest on at least three independent pieces of evidence from at least two
pieces of work, and E on a repeated pattern. The department SHALL NOT set a quota or forced distribution. A tier
SHALL NOT be cited without its period, scope, and record, and SHALL NOT be
published with a name, placed in a file an Agent reads, or computed
automatically.

#### Scenario: Two assessors disagree

- **WHEN** two assessors, judging separately from the original records, are
  two or more tiers apart
- **THEN** they record "insufficient evidence" and name what to examine next,
  without averaging or using half tiers
- **AND** an S adds an assessor from another domain, and the member may ask for
  one review with new evidence.

### Requirement: Promotion follows sustained evidence and carried responsibility

A member SHALL be promoted only after reaching S or A at their grade in at least
two consecutive periods while already carrying the next grade's responsibility
in their actual work; the count SHALL restart after a grade change. Entering
高级 and entering 专家 SHALL each require the crossing evidence the guidelines
name. Years, calendar time, and a single high score SHALL NOT replace that
evidence.

#### Scenario: A member is proposed for 高级

- **WHEN** a promotion to 高级 is reviewed
- **THEN** the record shows, in at least two different pieces of work, a method
  the member designed that another person adopted, that ran a full cycle, and
  that held when conditions changed
- **AND** the department reviews the number of periods after the first year of
  use.

### Requirement: Hiring reads the same grades

A grade's description SHALL be its hiring bar. Interview evidence SHALL set only
the entry grade; interview scores SHOULD be read against the target grade and
SHALL NOT convert into a tier. The first tier SHALL come from the first period
in post.

#### Scenario: A strong candidate is offered an S at hiring

- **WHEN** an interview panel proposes an S for a candidate
- **THEN** the candidate joins at the grade the evidence supports, with no tier
- **AND** the first tier comes from the first period in post.
