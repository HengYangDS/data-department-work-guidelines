## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- The same grade and the same records lead every assessor to the same tier.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any change to what a tier means, to the grade table's rows, or to promotion.

## Decisions

- **At-least-one reading.** C and B each ask for at least one result per requirement, matching D's first condition
  ("in any of the period's work"), so a period meets C, B or one of D's conditions and none falls between.
- **Next-row additions only.** A reads what the next row's "What the grade shows" adds, S what its "Responsibility it
  can carry" adds; what the member's own row asks counts toward B.
- **B against S.** Since S does not require A, assessors at B and S who both answer A's question no are reconciled as
  adjacent tiers.
- **Smaller wording points** go to the department's review after the first two periods, which reads agreement at
  every boundary.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The text is longer; each added sentence answers a case on which two readers split.
