# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Department guidance is readable without maintenance tools

Members and Agents SHALL be able to read and apply the task map and topics
without installing Node, npm, native checkers, or a maintenance bundle. The
repository SHALL distinguish reading guidance from maintaining its source.

#### Scenario: A reader opens the repository

- **WHEN** a member or Agent follows an entrypoint to a work question
- **THEN** the relevant English guidance is directly available as source or
  ordinary Forge-rendered Markdown
- **AND** no tool installation, local cache, or private host path is required.

#### Scenario: A maintainer needs source checks

- **WHEN** a contributor needs to change and verify the documentation
- **THEN** the contribution route points to the accepted installed product and
  the necessary DDWG native parameters
- **AND** setup is not presented as a prerequisite for reading or department work.

### Requirement: DDWG maintenance selects accepted product supply

DDWG SHALL consume the exact accepted ETHOS maintenance supply through its
native contract. Local verification and supplied offline installation SHALL
remain independent of either Forge. Product identity, supported platforms,
licenses, diagnostics, and applicable risk dispositions SHALL be qualified at
their actual owners, without another DDWG manifest or generic bundle product.

#### Scenario: A clean consumer receives the declared supply

- **WHEN** a supported clean environment receives the accepted product supply
- **THEN** its native installation and complete DDWG checks execute on the
  declared source with outbound access refused and no inherited cache or credentials
- **AND** the product's original identity, license, integrity, and platform
  checks remain applicable; a dry-run is not installation evidence.

#### Scenario: Supply is missing or altered

- **WHEN** a required product input is absent, changed, or unsupported
- **THEN** the native supply owner refuses before accepting the consumer
- **AND** it preserves the actual failure without downloading a substitute or
  weakening DDWG admission or quality boundary.

#### Scenario: A previous artifact approval exists

- **WHEN** a retained development artifact has an exact authorized disposition
- **THEN** its original subject, scope, and expiry remain unchanged
- **AND** that approval neither covers another repository or changed artifact
  nor requires the final product to retain the old tool or runtime.

#### Scenario: The shared supply replaces the old bundle

- **WHEN** the accepted product qualifies every current replacement consumer
- **THEN** DDWG retires its installer, bundle code, unused package inputs, shared
  manifests, duplicate supply specifications, and current setup instructions
- **AND** the former bundle is removed when its named consumer releases it;
  document editions identify shared supply rather than publish another tool product.

## REMOVED Requirements

**Reason:** Tool versions, acquisition, compatibility, offline installation,
audit execution, and supply algorithms have shared product owners. Reading is
the department's concern; creating another distribution plane is not.

**Migration:** Task 2.38 and its dependent qualifications exercise the accepted
installed supply, including retained platform and risk cases, before same-batch
retirement. Original cases and failures remain in native captured source evidence
`640cc1a0f708b9b8411d714f07d084376a8b691469e8518181d213366ab753e6`.
No historical Node, npm, or bundle choice is a required final implementation.
