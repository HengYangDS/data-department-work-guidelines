# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: DDWG source selects one English reader corpus

DDWG SHALL select its current entrypoints, seven department topics, decisions,
maintenance guidance, and retained source text through the accepted native
content contract. English and Department terminology SHALL be declared
parameters. Shared tools SHALL enforce their applicable checks; human review
SHALL assess faithful meaning, clarity, and reading quality.

#### Scenario: A member or Agent reads a current topic

- **WHEN** the reader follows the task map to a department work question
- **THEN** one English topic owns its duties, authority, conditions, and limits
- **AND** machine metadata is declared as native YAML front matter for the
  accepted registry, and the H1 starts the document body.

#### Scenario: A candidate changes the language or meaning

- **WHEN** selected current source reintroduces Chinese prose or changes a duty
- **THEN** the shared language check rejects the wrong language and the
  existing original-duty review assesses the meaning change
- **AND** a passing language or style report does not certify translation,
  complete equivalence, or team use.

#### Scenario: Historical source is consulted

- **WHEN** a retained original or earlier commit supports a clause review
- **THEN** it remains a historical input rather than current authority
- **AND** present translation or normalization does not certify its earlier
  language, execution, or lifecycle retrospectively.

### Requirement: Shared quality is consumed from the installed product

DDWG SHALL bind the complete applicable quality floor through accepted installed
ETHOS interfaces. Its durable specifications SHALL retain only department
requirements, native parameters, and justified business-specific increments.
Common standards and implementations SHALL remain at their product owners.
Actual consumer acceptance SHALL precede same-batch predecessor retirement.

#### Scenario: The installed consumer checks real repository source

- **WHEN** the shared check runs against the final selected DDWG source
- **THEN** it accepts corrected source and rejects a representative real defect
  through its official native format, lint, prose, link, and document interfaces
- **AND** source selection, meaningful literal bytes, and original diagnostics
  remain intact without an ETHOS source checkout or copied adopter executor.

#### Scenario: A current decision is validated

- **WHEN** the accepted shared consumer reads a current decision record
- **THEN** it enforces the requested stable lowercase identity and five-section
  form through its shared definition, without execution logs or empty rationale
- **AND** ordinary concepts and evidence links remain valid; DDWG does not
  reproduce the parser, shared section schema, or common decision standard.

#### Scenario: A packaged interface is incomplete

- **WHEN** the consumer lacks a recipe, native dependency, or correct input binding
- **THEN** the ETHOS owner receives the precise executable gap
- **AND** DDWG preserves its complete current floor and required failures without
  an alias, wrapper, extra installation, or copied common specification.

#### Scenario: A shared replacement is accepted

- **WHEN** the actual installed consumer satisfies the complete replaced duty
- **THEN** the same admitted transition retires duplicate code, generic tests,
  dependencies, common configuration, commands, guidance, and standard copies
- **AND** only content, necessary parameters, and a demonstrated unique increment
  remain; a local check does not establish broader product or team acceptance.
